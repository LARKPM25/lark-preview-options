craftsmanship

The Lark Difference

More than fifty years of American metal fabrication stand behind every kitchen — built by skilled craftsmen in the USA, from materials chosen to live outside for good.

It will not rust

A weather-resistant aluminum framework with stainless steel — everything corrosion-resistant, down to the hardware.

Five-year warranty

Metal materials and applied finishes are covered by a five-year limited warranty for the original purchaser.

Made in the USA

Every kitchen is made in America by skilled craftsmen — and installs quickly enough to enjoy the same day.

Construction

Light where it helps. Strong where it counts.

Lightweight aluminum construction and a modular design make these kitchens portable enough for rooftops and balconies — and strong enough for decades of dinners. Most ship fully assembled; when knocked-down shipping makes more sense, a laser-cut cleat system puts assembly at about 20–30 minutes.

Color-matched leveling legs make leveling simple and eliminate the need for a skirt or toe kick. And if you ever move, the kitchen is designed to disassemble and re-install with ease.

Storage that seals

Dry storage means dry storage.

Weatherproof stainless storage components with corrosion-resistant framework and hardware — and dry storage options that protect what's inside from moisture, humidity, and insects. Delivered pre-assembled with your kitchen, so every component fits perfectly.
