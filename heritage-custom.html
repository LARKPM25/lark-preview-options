How custom works

A design process built around what you already own and how you like to host.

1 · Share your space

Send your measurements, your layout idea, and photos of your patio, deck, rooftop, or poolside space.

2 · Design together

Our experienced design team works with you every step of the way — any size or layout, drawn to your specs.

3 · Built and delivered

Typical production runs about 4–6 weeks. Most kitchens ship fully assembled, ready to enjoy the same day.

Built around your gear

Bring your own grill. And fridge. And smoker.

Grills and appliances aren't included — on purpose. Send us the specs of your grill, smoker, or existing components and appliances, and the kitchen is designed to fit them exactly. Already own cabinet or drawer components you love? We can build around those too.
